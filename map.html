Directions & local pickup

Carbondale, PA - open by confirmed appointment only. BuccaneerSalvage ships auto parts nationwide from a private residential property, not a retail counter or walk-in salvage yard. Please confirm a time window before you drive.

How to book your visit

Picking up an auto part: purchase the item in our Store or message us via Contact with the part number first. Once your part is pulled and we confirm a time slot, use the map below to route into Carbondale, PA.

Scrap metal or electronics drop-off: schedule your slot on Scrap Removal (our direct haul phone line is posted there). Scheduled drop-off costs less than truck pickup on paid e-waste loads.
