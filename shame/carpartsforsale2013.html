What the listing already said

Condition notes on the live listing: WITOL 2M319J microwave magnetron, OEM, tested working. The part was bench-tested before shipment and the listing photos were of the actual unit.

A used magnetron that is tested and ships as described is not a mystery part. If it arrives damaged in transit, that is a shipping claim. If it does not fix the buyer’s microwave, that is a mismatch between symptom and part — not a defective listing.

What the buyer wrote

Nothing. No eBay message, no request for help, no description of what the microwave did or did not do. The return reason is the stock eBay selection “Doesn’t work or defective” with no further explanation.

No communication from buyer.

carpartsforsale2013 · return 5327339426 · Aug 2026

What we did

  1. Shipped the tested magnetron with tracking on the order.
  2. Offered a $7 partial refund to keep the part — more than 40% of the $17 purchase price — rather than force a return over a part that left here working.
  3. The buyer refused the keep-it offer and opened a return for the full amount after the 30-day window had passed.
  4. We reported the buyer and escalated the return to a case. We are not funding a free return label for a no-explanation claim on a tested pull.

What eBay did

eBay auto-approved return 5327339426 under the listing’s return policy. The return later escalated to a case for eBay review. Auto-approval is not a finding that the part was defective; it is the standard return-machine behavior once a buyer selects a reason. The case reference is return ID 5327339426.

How you stay off this wall

Ask for help. If a tested part does not solve your problem, tell us what the appliance is actually doing and we will troubleshoot. If you simply changed your mind or ordered the wrong part, pay to send it back. A stock “doesn’t work” reason with no details is not a warranty claim — it is a free-label request.
