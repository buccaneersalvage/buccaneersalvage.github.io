Who sails this ship

BuccaneerSalvage is run in Carbondale, PA by Cap’n Jules the Rustjack. We stock new-old-stock shelf inventory, tested used OEM pulls, and vintage hardware.

Every listing shows the actual part, its stamped or boxed part number, honest condition notes, and the return rules. Orders ship in 1–2 business days. We work from a private address by confirmed appointment only, not a walk-in yard. Questions about fitment or an order go through the contact form.
