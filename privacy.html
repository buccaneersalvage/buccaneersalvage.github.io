Privacy, short and honest

What this site collects. Spoiler: almost nothing.

What we collect

Nothing on your end. This site has no accounts, no logins, no newsletters, and no tracking cookies or analytics of its own. The optional contact form is processed by Formspree and emailed to us — name, email, phone, and message you choose to send. It is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server access logs (IP address, browser, requested page) as part of hosting — we don't see or use them.

Checkout and payment

All payments are processed off-site by Square (or eBay, for eBay orders). We never see or store your card number. Any name, address, email, or order details you enter at checkout are handled under Square's (or eBay's) privacy policy, and shared with us only as needed to ship your order.
