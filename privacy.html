Privacy policy

Plain-English summary of what this website collects, stores, and shares - and what it does not.

1. No analytics, ad pixels, or accounts

BuccaneerSalvage runs no analytics scripts (no Google Analytics, Plausible, or session recorders), no advertising pixels, no marketing cookies, and no customer login accounts. Fonts are self-hosted on this domain rather than fetched from Google Fonts.

2. Contact and service intake forms (Formspree)

When you submit a message on Contact or eBay services, the fields you fill in (such as your name, email address, optional phone number, topic, store URL, and message) plus the page URL are sent over HTTPS to Formspree and delivered to our Carbondale, PA inbox so we can reply. We use that information only to answer your inquiry or fulfill your order, and we never sell or rent contact details.

3. Shopping cart storage in your browser

When you add parts to your cart in the Store, your browser saves the selected item IDs, part titles, prices, thumbnails, and quantities in local browser storage (localStorage key buc-cart-v1). That list stays on your device until you clear your cart or complete checkout; it is not used to track you across sites.

4. Checkout and payments (Square & eBay)

We never see, process, or store your payment card number on this site:

  • Single-item links open hosted Square checkout directly.
  • Multi-item cart checkout sends only the selected Square catalog variation IDs and quantities to our checkout relay Worker (buc-square-checkout.jollyroger1480.workers.dev), which asks Square to create a combined hosted payment link and redirects your browser to Square.
  • Shipping and receipt details you enter on Square (or on eBay, for secondary eBay store orders) are governed by Square’s (or eBay’s) privacy policy and shared with us solely to pack, ship, or schedule local pickup for your order and keep required tax/accounting records.

5. Embedded maps and external media

Most pages load only first-party files and product photos hosted on Square’s image CDN. Two optional pages load external embeds when you visit them:

  • Directions & map embeds a Google Maps frame to show our Carbondale, PA area.
  • Music & videos loads video thumbnail images from YouTube (img.youtube.com) and links to YouTube when you click a video.

Loading those embeds transmits standard request data (such as IP address and browser user-agent) to Google/YouTube under their privacy terms.

6. Hosting logs (GitHub Pages)

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which collects standard web server access logs (such as visitor IP addresses, timestamps, and requested paths) for security and network reliability under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive raw visitor IP logs from GitHub Pages.

7. Contact & data requests

To ask a privacy question or request deletion of a message you sent through our contact forms, reach us through Contact.
