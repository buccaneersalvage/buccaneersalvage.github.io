FAQ

Eight questions, straight answers

Do you only work on auto parts?

Parts, tools, vintage hardware, and anything with an MPN are the strongest lane - that's where verified fitment earns its price. Vintage, collectibles, and home goods are accepted at Core rates.

Do you need my eBay password?

No. OAuth token only, scoped, revoke from your own Seller Hub, full audit log. And OAuth is optional - the default flow is draft-then-you-publish.

What if the catalog data is wrong?

One revision included when the data conflicts with your photos or the box. Changed-MPN research is a new SKU. I never invent a fitment row to fill a table - an honest gap beats a wrong row.

I have 400 listings. Can you handle that?

That's the catalog tier - $299/25 or $549/50 Core SKUs, CSV or batched pushes with GetItem verification. Same-MPN-family variants run cheaper. Unique-research SKUs eat a full slot; repeats in the same MPN family do not.

Can I send a spreadsheet instead of photos?

Core listings: yes - brand, MPN, condition, quantity in a CSV works. Full Builds need item photos for the description and condition notes.

Do you set my prices?

No - pricing strategy is out of scope, in writing. You own pricing; I own everything else on the listing form.

What if eBay rejects the page you build?

It won't - I only use the safe stuff eBay allows. If eBay ever changes its rules down the road, we'd fix it at the normal rate.

Can I cancel mid-month?

Anytime, no lock-in. Unused listings bank one month (max 15). Pause 30 days once per 6 months. Retainer months are billed 50% to start, remainder after the first batch.
