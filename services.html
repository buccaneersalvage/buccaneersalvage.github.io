FAQ

Eight questions, straight answers

Do you only work on auto parts?

Parts, tools, and vintage hardware are our strongest lane because catalog fitment matters most there. Vintage, collectibles, and home goods are welcome at the same $2.50 / $5 rates.

Do you need my eBay password?

No. Default delivery is a draft you paste in yourself. Optional OAuth publishing is granted from your own eBay settings and can be revoked anytime.

What if the catalog data is wrong?

One revision is included when catalog data conflicts with your photos or box. A changed part number is a new listing. We never invent fitment rows.

I have 400 listings. Can you handle that?

In batches: $50 for 25 text listings, $90 for 50. Similar MPN-family variants cost less than unique research.

Can I send a spreadsheet instead of photos?

Yes for text listings: brand, part number, condition, and quantity. Styled listings require photos of the actual item.

Do you set my prices?

No. You set prices; we prepare the title, specifics, fitment, and description.

What if eBay rejects the HTML page?

We only use eBay-compliant static HTML/CSS (zero scripts or forms). Monthly plan listings get free rejection fixes; later platform policy changes are quoted at standard rates.

Can I cancel mid-month?

Yes. You pay only for work already delivered. Unused monthly listings roll forward one month, and you can pause 30 days once every 6 months.
