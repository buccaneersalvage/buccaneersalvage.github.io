FAQ

Eight questions, straight answers

Do you only work on auto parts?

Parts, tools, and vintage hardware are the strongest lane because that's where catalog fitment matters. Vintage, collectibles, and home goods are fine at Core rates.

Do you need my eBay password?

No. Default is a draft you paste in yourself. If you later want me to push, it's a token you revoke from your own Seller Hub. No shared passwords.

What if the catalog data is wrong?

One revision when the catalog fights your photos or the box. A changed part number is a new SKU. I don't invent a fitment row to fill a table. An honest gap beats a wrong row.

I have 400 listings. Can you handle that?

Catalog batches: $35 for 25 Core SKUs, $59 for 50. A 400-SKU store is several batches, not one dump. Same-family variants cost less than unique research.

Can I send a spreadsheet instead of photos?

Core: yes. Brand, part number, condition, quantity. Full Builds need photos of the actual item.

Do you set my prices?

No. You set prices. I fill the rest of the form.

What if eBay rejects the page you build?

I only use markup eBay allows: no scripts, no forms. If they change the rules later, a fix is quoted at the normal rate.

Can I cancel mid-month?

Yes. You pay for work already delivered. Unused listings from that month roll one more month. Pause 30 days once per 6 months. Maintain $29/mo is billed through Square on subscribe, then monthly.
