What this service does - and does not do

Rustjack Systems provides design, automation, and data services for eBay sellers. The scope of every engagement is limited to: building listing templates, populating Item Specifics, producing structured fitment / specification data, and (with explicit OAuth permission) pushing changes to a seller’s eBay account via the Trading API.

What we do not promise: we do not guarantee any specific level of sales, conversion rate, search rank, watch count, BSR, click-through rate, or any other commercial outcome. eBay’s Cassini algorithm and buyer behavior are not under our control. Improved design, accurate data, and clean compliance may improve visibility, but we never promise they will.

We are not responsible for: data accuracy of third-party catalogs and manufacturer data used as research sources; changes to eBay’s HTML policies, active content rules, or category requirements after a listing is published; seller-side errors; or any loss of business, fees, account standing, or disputes arising from listings we create or revise.

All content and design work is provided “as-is.” By engaging Rustjack Systems, you agree that our liability is limited to the fees paid for the work, and that any dispute is governed by Pennsylvania law. Full terms: terms-service.html.
