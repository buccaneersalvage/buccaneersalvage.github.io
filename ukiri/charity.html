Ukiri Fraud Report / Charity Record

Nonprofit filings for EIN 93-1755201

Ukiri Global Foundation: state incorporation and IRS $0 filings

Promoted in interviews and features as a charity started in 2016, yet Indiana corporate filings date to May 25, 2023 and public IRS extracts show $0.

Donor warning

Incorporated in May 2023 with $0 reported on IRS filings

Ukiri told a defense investigator in August 2025 that his foundation was set up in 2016 (see the 2025 memo). AAA Hoosier Magazine later repeated a similar “started in 2016” story. Indiana corporate filings and federal IRS records tell a very different story.

Public records for Ukiri Global Foundation Inc (EIN 93-1755201), registered at the exact same shop address, 2910 Main St, Anderson, IN 46016-5248, show:

  • Not legally incorporated as an Indiana nonprofit until May 25, 2023 (INBiz Business ID 202305251694548), listing incorporators Joseph Ukiri and Christopher Hiday. Earlier web domains or social pages were not a registered nonprofit corporation.
  • Not recognized as a federal IRS 501(c)(3) until June 1, 2023 (IRS ruling period 202306), roughly 18 months after my mother’s December 2021 $120,000 check went to the auto shop.
  • Zero dollars reported on public IRS extracts: the organization files Form 990-N (e-Postcard) only. The IRS public extract lists Assets $0, Income $0, and Revenue $0, with zero Form 990 financial schedules and no public tax record of funded charity programs.
  • Public registration details: NTEE category O50 (Youth Development), listed phone 765-610-7277, and contact email pattern info@ukiriglobalfoundations.org.

Warning to prospective donors: Before May 25, 2023, no Indiana nonprofit corporation existed on file under this name. Before June 1, 2023, it held no federal 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status. Do not send checks or donations here based on “since 2016” stories on Facebook, ReroTV clips, magazine features, or shop flyers.
