Jossy’s Auto: the dealership on the $120,000 check

If an online romance partner or contact tells you to send money to a car lot in Anderson, Indiana, stop and verify the state enforcement and shipping records below.

Check destination

Where my mother’s $120,000 cashier’s check landed

My mother did not mail a check into thin air. She sent it to a registered Indiana auto dealership: Jossy’s Auto at 2910 Main Street, Anderson, Indiana 46016 (operated by Joseph “Jossy” Ukiri / Jossy’s Autos LLC). That is the exact street address on the shipping trail, and that is where her $120,000 cashier’s check (dated December 8, 2021, delivered December 9, 2021, and signed for by Michael Devol) was received.

When questioned, Ukiri claimed he was an innocent go-between helping a man named “Yusuf” buy cars at Copart, and insisted the check was handed to him in person (see his 2025 interview memo). The documents show the opposite: USPS Priority Overnight delivery records prove it arrived by mail, a Copart subpoena returned zero cars for that alibi, large withdrawals drained the funds within days, and the overseas scammer demanded silence as soon as I named the dealership online.
