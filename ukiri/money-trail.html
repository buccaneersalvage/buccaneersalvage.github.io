Ukiri Fraud Report / Money Trail

Where the Money Went

Rapid cash-outs and the Nigeria sand-dredge trail

Subpoenaed Copart records showed zero cars for Ukiri’s alibi, while bank records showed rapid December 2021 withdrawals and public posts marketed dredgers to Nigeria.

Step 1 · December 2021

What happened after the $120,000 check hit Jossy’s Auto

The cashier’s check memo line read “Inventory Capital.” Once deposited, the money moved out of the business path within days, including documented December 2021 withdrawals of roughly $46.7k, $23.7k, and $49.5k. Subpoenaed Copart auction records show zero cars purchased for Ukiri’s “Yusuf” vehicle-export story.

Instead, Ukiri posted in the Facebook group Nigerian Dredge Masters and Engineers Association (NDMEA): “American direct dredger for sale. Arriving Nigeria in March.” That listing matches portable American hydraulic dredgers (the IMS Versi-Dredge equipment class commonly exported to Nigeria), whose market price overlaps a six-figure check.

Step 2 · After July 2023

Marketing a $500k-class Nigeria dredge after his FBI interview

In July 2023, the FBI interviewed Ukiri and recorded his claim that he was merely a victim or middleman (FD-302 series). Public advocacy records show that roughly 7 months later, he was back on Facebook promoting a sand dredge export to Nigeria (~$500k class), with Texas IRONPlanet government-auction sourcing cited in contemporaneous reports sent to senators and prosecutors (see congressional inquiry).

He was marketing heavy industrial equipment overseas while federal agents already had his file open. Case records also note family travel to Nigeria in December 2023, after the July 2023 FBI interview and cell-phone download window.

Compliance & victim warning

Why banks, donors, and other families should pay attention

An auto lot that takes a $120,000 romance-scam cashier’s check, a foundation at the same address that only gained IRS 501(c)(3) status in June 2023 with $0 on its Form 990-N extract, and parallel overseas sand-dredge marketing are red flags that belong in daylight. If you are another victim, a bank BSA investigator, a reporter, or a relative watching someone get asked to “help with inventory” or “donate to the foundation,” save every record, stop payment, and get in touch.
