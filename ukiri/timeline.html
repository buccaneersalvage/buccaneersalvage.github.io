2020 – 2021

Romance and advance-fee scam targets my mother. Scammer personas include “Austin S. Miller” using genaustinsmiller219@gmail.com (see chat record).

Dec 8, 2021

$120,000 cashier’s check is issued and mailed by USPS Priority Overnight to Jossy’s Auto in Anderson, Indiana (delivery contradiction).

Dec 9, 2021

Overnight delivery and rapid withdrawals: USPS package is signed for by Michael Devol (shop record). Funds enter Ukiri’s business banking path and leave in large December tranches (~$46.7k, ~$23.7k, ~$49.5k).

Dec 29–30, 2022

Same-window police report and silence demand: Ukiri files a report with Henry County PD (CAD 819462). Within the same window, the scammer messages my mother demanding she stop my online posts (recorded in sworn IC3 and FD-302 filings; read verbatim message).

Jan 4, 2023

Indiana SOS dealer complaint #CMP-2301-0012 (filed by me, Julian Deuerlein) is received by the state. Auto Dealer Services opens a field audit of Jossy’s Autos 13 days later on Jan 17, 2023 (dealer table).

Feb 27, 2023

2023 dealer license suspension and Order for Restitution DLR-2302-0066 (Indiana SOS Auto Dealer Services): state investigators find Jossy’s charged 15 buyers bogus $18 and $20 “tag and title” fees (where the legal fee was $15 and no titling service was provided). Restitution is ordered, with personal service listed in Middletown, IN (shop record).

May 25 · Jun 1, 2023

Ukiri Global Foundation is legally incorporated as an Indiana nonprofit on 05/25/2023 (INBiz ID 202305251694548) and granted federal 501(c)(3) status on 06/01/2023, roughly 18 months after the $120,000 check. Subsequent IRS public extracts show $0 assets, $0 income, and $0 revenue (Form 990-N only) (charity filings).

Jul 2023

The FBI interviews Ukiri (FD-302, July 13, 2023), where he claims victim or middleman status. The same FD-302 records that an Indiana BMV senior inspector had already warned him about vehicle-export scalping / scouting, and documents accounts ending 8004 and 8012 plus a second-payor trail (second money trail). Family travel to Nigeria follows in Dec 2023 (money trail).

Jul 11, 2024

I am arrested (Julian Deuerlein) in a separate federal case roughly two weeks after my public complaints about FBI inaction on the Ukiri fraud, the sequence behind the retaliation theory noted on the Evidence page.

Sep 2024 →

Public YouTube evidence walkthroughs and screenshots are published regarding Joseph “Jossy” Ukiri and Jossy’s Auto (@BuccaneerSalvage). Ukiri’s wife is not part of this public warning, and no evidence section on this site is directed against her.

Mar 19, 2025

Federal suppression hearing: Special Agent Ivan Torres testifies in substance that he believed Ukiri or Jossy’s Autos took the money. Ukiri is not arrested (testimony summary).

Aug 5–6, 2025

Defense investigator John R. Brunza interviews Ukiri at Jossy’s Auto; his memo records Ukiri’s “never received any bank check in the mail” claim, Copart story, foundation claims, and “$85k+ lost income” statement (memo breakdown).

2025

Copart subpoena return confirms zero vehicle purchases matching Ukiri’s export-car alibi (alibi collapse), while the separate federal case proceeds on its own track.

Dec 2025

AAA Hoosier Magazine features Jossy’s Auto and Ukiri Global Foundation, repeating the foundation’s promotional narrative (compared against IRS filings).

Jan – Jul 2026

Ongoing public reporting, White House correspondence, Sen. John Fetterman’s congressional inquiry (established Feb 19; FBI stall replies May 7 and July 10, 2026), and publication of this multi-page record.

Jul 14, 2026 · Latest enforcement

Indiana Secretary of State records a second suspension on Jossy’s Autos LLC Dealer #2000345 (status date 7/14/2026), verified live on dealers.sos.in.gov. The official written order was not yet posted on AAOnline as of last check (Indiana Auto Dealer Services: (317) 234-7190). This follows the 2023 suspension, restitution order DLR-2302-0066, and the BMV scalping/scouting warning.
