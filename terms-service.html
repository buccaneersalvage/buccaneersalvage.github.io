01

Scope of services

Rustjack Systems (a service mark of BuccaneerSalvage, operated by Cap'n Jules the Rustjack, Carbondale PA 18407) provides the following services to eBay sellers:

  • Custom eBay listing HTML templates, designed to client specifications
  • Item Specifics population and SEO-friendly title generation
  • Vehicle fitment research (auto parts) and product specification research (other categories), drawing from official distributor catalogs, manufacturer data sheets, and curated cross-reference databases
  • Bulk listing pipelines: spreadsheet or batched pushes, with a dry-run first and a check after they land
  • Listing audits, compliance review, and on-page fixes
  • OAuth account access (read+write) to a seller's eBay account, granted voluntarily and revocable at any time
  • Web design and site-building for sellers who also want a Squarespace / Shopify / static-HTML storefront alongside their eBay presence

Every engagement begins with a written scope agreement (email is sufficient) that defines deliverables, fees, and timeline. Work outside that scope requires a new agreement.

OAuth account access

If you grant us OAuth access to your eBay account:

  • You retain full ownership of the account, all listings, and all data
  • You may revoke access at any time from your eBay account settings. We lose the ability to push changes the moment you do
  • Every change we make is logged via the Trading API's ReviseFixedPriceItem / AddFixedPriceItem audit trail, recoverable from your eBay seller hub
  • We will never change store policies, payment information, or payout details. Those are off-limits regardless of scope
  • We will never close or pause listings without your explicit, written approval (per-message basis, not blanket)
  • If you ask us to push bulk changes, we will always show you a dry-run report first unless you waive that in writing for a specific batch
You are responsible for reviewing every change we make. If a Revise or Add call lands and you do not like the result, contact us within 7 days and we will fix it free of charge or roll it back.
